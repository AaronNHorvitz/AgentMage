  - The largest completed output is 12578 tokens (dev-013, 291.5 s).

  At the observed decode rate, about 13.5K tokens is the most any task could produce within 300 s. The time limit
  therefore bounds every task below its allowance. Nothing published is false: 300/60 s is stated, and the 300.0xx s
  rows and timeout causes are visible. But a reader of the allowance column over-estimates this profile's effective
  room by roughly 2×. Fix (documentation only, in the next additive note): state that the 300 s total timeout cut the
  4 timeouts at about 13.0–13.5K generated tokens and bounds Muse's effective output near that level. Changing the
  timeout is T-309 backlog.
