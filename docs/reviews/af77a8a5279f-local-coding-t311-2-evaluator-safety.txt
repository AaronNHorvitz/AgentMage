- **L1: a coincident cleanup failure is not reflected in `report.json`.** At
  `scripts/coding_eval.py:268-278`, when the case body fails with an ordinary error and

  is correct. However, the cleanup failure is visible only through the separate private
  `cleanup.json`, and the report does not mention a leftover tree. The case still fails and has

  `cleanup.json` exists, so a reader of the report alone sees the leftover.
