## Findings

### M1 (medium): dev-018's capture stub has a different shape from the original fixture, so oracle and frozen-method verdicts diverge both ways

- Location: `scripts/coding_eval_oracle_worker.py:448-451` (candidate stub is always
  `subprocess.CompletedProcess([], returncode, stdout=…)`) and `:427-432` (the trusted proxy snapshots
  only `stdout`/`returncode` of whatever `subprocess.run.return_value` is). Coverage at
  `scripts/coding_eval_oracle.py:67-73` and the guide at `docs/guides/coding-evaluation-oracle.md:232-234`
  do not declare the difference.
- Reasoning: dev-005's hidden method stubs `run` with a real `CompletedProcess([], 0, stdout=…)`, so the
  recreated stub matches it exactly. dev-018's hidden method uses `patch.object(evidence.subprocess,
  "run") as run` with `run.return_value.stdout`/`.returncode` assigned. That completed process is a
  `MagicMock`: `.stderr`, `.args` and other attributes are mocks, `isinstance(…, CompletedProcess)` is
  false, and `str + mock` yields a mock. The oracle gives the candidate a `CompletedProcess` with
  `stderr=None`.
- Reproduction (reviewer probes above, reference source plus one suffix):
  - `text = (result.stdout + (result.stderr or "")).replace(...)`: independent oracle **PASS**, while
    the frozen original method under the legacy grader **FAILS** (1 failure).
  - `str(result.stderr.replace("x", "y"))`: oracle FAILS as `candidate_exception`, legacy PASSES.
  - For dev-005 both graders agree on both sources.
- Impact: the row requires the complete original methods to judge the candidate on the original
  fixture. For dev-018 the oracle can grant a pass the frozen hidden method would deny, and the reverse
  is labelled as a candidate exception. The report's `coverage` gives no sign of this. Under the
  evaluation-integrity directive, an undeclared lenient divergence in a grader meant to precede
  generated scoring blocks closing the row. It is not high because generated scoring is disabled and no
  score exists.
- Fix (either):
  - (a) Preferred: transport a closed stub kind from the trusted proxy. Use `"completed-process"` when
    `type(run.return_value) is subprocess.CompletedProcess`, also carrying `args == []` and
    `stderr is None` or refusing. Use `"mock"` when `run` is a `unittest.mock` mock. In the candidate,
    rebuild the matching stub, e.g. `with mock.patch.object(module.subprocess, "run") as run:
    run.return_value.stdout = …; run.return_value.returncode = …`. Refuse any other shape as trusted
    protocol.
  - (b) Otherwise, declare `completed-process-attributes-beyond-stdout-returncode` as `not_graded` and
    `mock-stub-semantics` as adapter policy for dev-018, and stop describing it as the test's stub.
  - Either way, add regression controls equivalent to the two dev-018 probes above, with the expected
    legacy contrast.
