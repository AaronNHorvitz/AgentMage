## L5 verification: the leader stays unreaped through both group signals

Code reviewed: `scripts/local_agent_fast_gate.py:171-189` (`observe_exit`,
`stop_check`) and `:219` (`supervise`).

- `observe_exit` uses `os.waitid(P_PID, pid, WEXITED | WNOHANG | WNOWAIT)`.
  `CLD_EXITED` maps to the exit status. Killed or dumped maps to the negated
  signal, and `supervise` turns that into `128 + signal`.
- `stop_check` sends TERM to the group. It then polls `observe_exit`, without
  reaping, for up to 5 s, sends KILL to the group, and only then calls
  `process.wait()`. Nothing else in the supervisor reaps. I checked that there is
  no Popen context manager, `poll()` or `communicate()` and no SIGCHLD disposition
  change.
- **Timing, unchanged.** After a normal exit, KILL follows TERM immediately, as it
  did before the repair. Members that ignore TERM therefore still get no grace
  period after the leader has exited. That matches the documented behaviour.

I wrote an independent in-process probe that wraps `os.killpg` and the reaping
calls while the committed `supervise()` runs. At every group signal it checks with
a separate `WNOWAIT` probe whether the leader is still waitable:

| Scenario | Return | Observed sequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| exit 0 | 0 | TERM (leader zombie), KILL (zombie), reap |
| exit 7 | 7 | TERM (zombie), KILL (zombie), reap |
| self-SIGTERM | 143 | TERM (zombie), KILL (zombie), reap |
| timeout, leader responds to TERM | 124 | TERM (alive), KILL (zombie), reap |
| timeout, leader ignores TERM | 124 | TERM (alive), KILL (alive, after 5 s), reap |
| gate lifeline EOF | 130 | TERM (alive), KILL (zombie), then the reap |

In every case the group ID stays reserved by the unreaped leader until both
signals have been sent.

### L6 (low): the review-text check accepts endings with two contradictory verdicts

Location: `scripts/local_agent_fast_gate.py:112-114`.

`final_verdict` only checks the last line. Reproduction in a temporary root:

- A report ending `Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE` and then `Verdict: PASS` is accepted.
- A report ending `Verdict: PASS` and then `Verdict: FINDINGS` is accepted.

Both contradict the reviewer format, which ends with one verdict line. The check is
documented as format-only and fails closed in every other case I tried, so this is
not a security or closure issue.

Fix: require exactly one line that equals `Verdict: PASS`, `Verdict: FINDINGS` or
`Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE`. For INCONCLUSIVE, also refuse a reason line that starts
with `Verdict:`. Add the two endings above as rejected cases. Both currently
retained reports contain exactly one `Verdict: ` line, so they remain valid.
