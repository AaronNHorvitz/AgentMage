## Findings

No critical, high or medium findings.

- LOW (test robustness): two new tests give themselves only 1 second of real time.
  - Where: repository_preservation_tests.rs:283 and repository_preservation_fs_tests.rs:317.
  - Problem: before the intended stop point, these tests do real work: up to 16 synthetic worker
    calls plus local scans, or a `Files` setup plus one 64 KiB chunk. On a heavily loaded host
    that work can take longer than 1 s. The test then fails falsely with `calls < stop` or
    `checks < 3`. It cannot produce a false pass.
  - Fix: use a larger budget, such as 10 s, so the sleep-to-deadline still expires at the
    intended point, or add a test-only clock seam.
- LOW (stale comment): local_commit_execution.rs:98 still says "collection retains its own worker
  deadlines". Collections now share one 30 s budget, capped at 15 s per worker. Fix: update the
  comment.
- LOW (coverage, acknowledged): no test runs `run_read_only` to show that `collection_deadline`
  reaches `Launch.deadline`.
  - Why: native worker execution is blocked in this lane by executable ownership.
  - What is covered: the calculation (`inspection_deadline`) and the forwarding of
    `request.deadline` are tested directly. I verified the two-line wiring by reading it
    (repository_safety.rs:542, :683).
  - Backlog: add a seam test, or a native run once ownership is mapped.
