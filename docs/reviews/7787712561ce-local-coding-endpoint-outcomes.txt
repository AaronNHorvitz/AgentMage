- **L1 (low, backlog): "current turn" means only the supplied ordinal.**
  - Location: `kernel/engine/src/runtime_coordinator/endpoint_outcome.rs:150-161`, prose at
    `docs/evidence/t303-endpoint-outcomes-2026-11-08.md` ("the claim's actual current turn") and
    `docs/guides/runtime-endpoint-runs.md` ("must identify that claim's actual turn").
  - The check binds the claim's turn id to the caller-supplied `turn_count`. It cannot see the
    `RuntimeEndpointTurns` ledger, so it cannot establish that the claim is the latest accepted turn.
  - Reproduction: the reviewer probe above shows that, after two accepted turns, a turn-1 claim with
    counts 1/1 seals.
  - No authority is granted, because the coordinator still decides, so this is not a boundary
    defect.
  - Fix at full coordinator composition: derive `turn_count` and `model_call_count` from the owning
    turns component (or pass it), and add a test with a stale claim after a later turn. Until then,
    reword the prose to "the turn identified by the supplied turn count".

  a finding.
