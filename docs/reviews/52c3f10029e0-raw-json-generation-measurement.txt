### M1 (medium): the published refusal subclassification understates Markdown fencing (25/25, not 19/25)

- Locations: docs/evidence/t303-raw-json-generation-2026-11-12.md:97 and docs/evidence/coding-scoreboard.md:965
  ("classifies 19 of those answers as JSON code fences and six as other JSON-format failures"). The source is
  archived classify_answers.py:10:
  `fenced=len(lines)>=2 and lines[0].strip()=='```json' and lines[-1].strip()=='```'`.
- Reproduction: in the archived answers, every one of the six "other_json_format" answers (dev-012, dev-013,
  dev-014, dev-021, dev-022, dev-027) begins with "```json\n{\"edits\":…". Their closing fence is joined to the
  last line, e.g. `…}]}```` or `…}]})````, so the last-line test misses it. Counting answers whose first
  non-blank characters are ```` ``` ```` gives 25/30. That is all 25 format refusals. The 5 bare answers are
  the 4 accepted proposals and the dev-025 edit refusal.
- Why medium: this batch's only changed variable is an instruction that ends "without Markdown fences".
  The published line implies fencing fell from 30/30 to 19/30 answers. The raw logs show 30/30 to 25/30,
  and every remaining format refusal is still fenced. This is the batch's main causal observation under
  MEASURE FIRST, and it will guide the next source step. As written, it is not supported by the raw logs it cites.
- Fix: report fence shape separately from closing-fence placement. For example: "all 25 format refusals begin
  with a ```json fence; 19 close it on its own line, six join the closing fence to the last line, and their
  JSON payloads are malformed or followed by extra characters". Correct classify_answers.py to detect a leading
  fence independently of closing placement, or add that field, and re-seal its output and binding. Since earlier
  scoreboard bytes are kept as-is, add the correction as a dated corrigendum line in coding-scoreboard.md, and
  update the evidence file and its "Answer syntax observations" binding. The counts of strict proposals and
