## Review of the claimed repairs

- **Daemon-store indicators (old L3)** (`local_commit_inspection.rs:92-100`).
  - `revalidate` now calls `statat(directory_fd, "public-keys.d" | "common.conf", SYMLINK_NOFOLLOW)` on the
    held descriptor. It passes only on `NOENT`; any entry (file, directory, dangling symlink, socket, empty
    file) or any other errno refuses. No content is read.
  - `revalidate` runs before database selection (`:103`, before the `openat` loop), before sealing (`:151`)
    and after sealing (`:155`). The old pre-check position was moved after `check()`, which is equivalent.
  - The test `signer_inspection_refuses_daemon_markers_even_with_a_valid_legacy_database` covers both markers
    in all four entry types, with both legacy names present, plus intentional empty-`common.conf` refusal.
  - The `create_at in [3, 4]` cases tell the two rechecks apart. With a 6-byte file, check #3 is after the
    read and check #4 is after sealing. `assert_eq!(checks, create_at)` fails if the pre-seal recheck is
    removed (refusal would move to check 4). Removing the post-seal recheck makes `create_at = 4` succeed.
  - The decision, guide and evidence state that refusal of every `common.conf` is intentional, and that
    capture is not atomic against same-user changes. Both statements are accurate.
- **Designated revokers (old L1)** (`:347-375`).
  - `pending` now carries `(eligible, is_primary)`. `rvk` neither consumes nor sets `pending`, never
    increments `matches`, requires a well-formed field 10 fingerprint, and refuses while a **subkey** is
    pending.
  - The selected fingerprint is still compared only on an `fpr` that directly consumes a pending `pub` or
    `sub`. A revoker therefore cannot qualify, cannot replace a missing primary `fpr` (the next `pub`/`sub`,
    or the end of input, sees `pending`), and cannot be placed before a subkey `fpr`.
  - The synthetic test checks all three cases, and its sub-key case does discriminate: without the
    interposed `rvk`, that subkey would match.
  - The strengthened installed-GnuPG fixture adds a real designated revoker. It asserts an `rvk` line,
    primary acceptance and revoker refusal. It then runs `inspect_with` with the production `gpg_arguments`
    for both primary and subkey over the sealed keybox and the exported `pubring.gpg` snapshots. The
    old parser would have refused the primary there, as my probe shows.
- **CleanupUncertain at both worker boundaries (old L2).**
  - Dispatch mapping is factored into `map_dispatch` (`:224-228`), and production `inspect` calls it
    (`:205`). The mapping (`capture_error(kind, ArtifactDenied)`) is unchanged.
  - The new test drives `map_dispatch(Err(..))` and `validate_result` with `outcome: Err(..)`. It covers
    both `CleanupUncertain` (preserved) and `InvalidManifest` (falls back to ArtifactDenied), plus
    `Ok` passthrough.
  - `LinuxSandboxError::for_test` (`sandbox.rs:185-188`) is `#[cfg(test)] pub(crate)`. Its only caller is
    this test module, and the struct still has a single private `kind` field. It grants no production
    authority.
  - As the evidence says, the test exercises the helper and not native worker cleanup.
