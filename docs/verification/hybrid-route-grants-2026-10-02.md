## Change

- The catalog host keeps one route grant catalog as the owner state `route-grant-catalog`. A
  grant lets one exact remote route of a named provider receive the listed data from one
  workspace's runs, for at most a number of requests and input tokens, until an expiry. The
  owner keeps the grant's digest, when it was granted, the person's decision digest, the
  counters and any revocation.
- A grant is accepted only within its rules:
  - its digest recomputes, and its identities, data classes and budgets are bounded;
  - its expiry falls within 30 days and an hour of the host's clock;
  - no other grant in the workspace has its identity, and no live grant in the workspace
    names its route.

  Revoking is permanent, and revoked and expired grants stay listed. A clock earlier than
  the catalog's is refused. A counting operation for a later remote adapter is offered and
  called nowhere.
- The CLI gains `--route-grant FILE`, `--route-grant-list` and `--route-grant-revoke
  GRANT_ID`, with `--route-grant-workspace ID`. A grant request file is read exactly and
  sealed from the client's clock. The grant is shown, as text or as one JSON row, and sent
  only after a typed `yes`. The harness gains `route-grant`.
- The development factory opens its store before routing. It routes in hybrid mode, under a
  fixed hybrid policy, when the run's workspace holds a live grant, and carries each live
  grant with its counters. It offers only the run's profile route, so the local route is
  selected. A remote selection would refuse the composition, because no remote adapter
  exists.
- The client keeps a hybrid receipt in two cases: it selects the run's own route, or it
  selects an eligible remote route named with its grant and provider. A fallback receipt is
  never kept. A remote selection is shown on its own line as `REMOTE route ... of provider
  ... under grant ...`, and its route history entry names the grant.
- The catalog operation crosses wire 17, and the development host refuses it. The runtime
  producer contract moves to version 3, with the same records over wire 17. Version 2 stays
  unchanged.
- TASKS.md gains AMR-05.9.7.1 and AMR-05.9.7.2, and the work selector covers 82 amendment
  rows.
