# Sprint 56 Local Verification

## Scope

The Sprint 56 local recorder covers sealed document and correspondence registers, six statement
truth classes, attachment and deadline review, ten local workflow reports, four exact file-action
previews, action-specific approval review, three runtime schemas, eight authority-free secretary
skills, required templates, and the closed 68-case acceptance corpus.

## Local Campaigns

- Register entries retain version, approval, attachments, commitments, deadlines, statements,
  hashes, source paths, named parties, quorum/status, category, retention, accessibility review,
  supersession, and exact source references.
- Verbatim source, observed fact, derived action, inferred summary, unresolved conflict, and
  user-approved final language remain distinct and cannot be silently promoted.
- Naming, duplicate, superseded, final-copy, quality, deadline, routing-slip, merge, calendar, and
  filing workflows produce local reports with zero external effects.
- Save, rename, move, and filing proposals require exact source/destination, content, metadata,
  category, retention, and sealed preview fields as applicable.
- Approval review binds the exact preview digest and confirmations but creates no execution grant.
- Unsupported identities, hidden recipients, malicious attachments, embedded instructions,
  sensitive content, and disposition requests create no authority.
