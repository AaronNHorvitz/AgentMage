## Change

The review fixes add the following:

- The job ledger keeps an owner reserve and a separate refusal bound, and replay must
  end at the retained head.
- The hunk display escapes every character as the whole-file preview does.
- The context builder refuses a first composition that omits a retained constraint.
- The CLI marks a truncated run and reads review targets relative to their parent
  without following links.
- The test-ownership scanner treats `name/mod.rs` modules as production.
- Retained stages are typed, checked for private names and verified against their
  source revision.

New open rows track the remaining native and view obligations.

A person can now answer a patch review with `select` and hunk numbers. The original
call is refused and never runs. The runtime proposes one separate, shell-only write of
the selected hunks, and that write needs its own approval and grant. The host
re-plans the original patch over the exact current bytes and refuses drift, stale
digests, unknown hunks, and empty or complete selections. A model cannot propose
the derived tool, and a derived write cannot be narrowed again.
