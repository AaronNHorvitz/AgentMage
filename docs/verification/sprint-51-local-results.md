The Sprint 51 local recorder covers deterministic task-tier selection, the six approved frontier
recommendation triggers, separation of user-owned clarification, exact local packet composition,
content minimization, disclosure inventory, stable review hashes, optional user-recorded destination
bookkeeping, and structural denial of every declared delivery path.

## Local Campaigns

- Thirteen tier cases cover deterministic success, an unattempted local model, verified local
  success, unmeasured outcomes, user-owned clarification, measured and unsupported local failure,
  both sides of the repeated-validation threshold, contradiction, rejected verification, exhausted
  budget, and a material external-expertise need.
- Ten disclosure cases reject credentials, private or unrelated files, hidden metadata, absolute
  paths, excessive excerpts, serialized authority, prompt injection, and missing current-state
  evidence before preview. A fully redacted bounded entry remains eligible for local review only.
- Fourteen delivery actions are closed across the Rust and TypeScript boundaries. Direct, injected,
  scheduled, routed, and standing-consent variants all produce local denials and no external effect.
- Eight review mutations cover workspace, policy, redaction policy, source content, packet bytes,
  manifest, disclosure inventory, and expiry. Changed bytes cannot reuse the reviewed hash.
- Runtime schemas close tier-decision and recommendation-receipt fields. Receipt validation binds the
  exact decision, packet, disclosure result, optional user-owned destination record, and false
  external-delivery marker.

workflow. No local source contract establishes live model failure, installed-platform acceptance,
