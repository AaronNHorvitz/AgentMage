# Sprint 54 Local Verification

## Scope

The Sprint 54 local recorder covers the closed executive-record grammar, immutable portfolio
reconciliation, deterministic priority explanations, six trackers, thirteen views, correspondence
draft review, local message-export triage, four privacy classes, plain-folder and Obsidian
projection parity, and eight authority-free declarative skills.

## Local Campaigns

- Nine ranking dimensions contribute visible integer points, reason codes, source identities, and
  limitations. Ties use stable record identity; unknown data and unresolved dependencies remain
  visible rather than guessed.
- Five evidence states remain distinct across trackers and briefs. Active or completed commitments
  and decisions require confirmed evidence, and sensitive-trait fields are rejected from person and
  organization briefs.
- Six tracker classes and thirteen view classes cover cycle, portfolio, status, meeting, decision,
  person, organization, pack, changes-since, forgotten-item, and audit projections.
- Seven correspondence checks and seven message-export triage classes execute with no recipient
  selection, inbox access, notification, sending, scheduling, network use, or file mutation.
- Source correction, supersession, completion, and disputed conflict reconciliation preserve the
  previous immutable records in history.
- Sixteen privacy cases cover retrieval, indexing, retention, and export for ordinary, private,
  confidential, and highly restricted records. Highly restricted indexing and export are denied.
- The same ranking and reminder projections pass for plain-folder and Obsidian source grammars.
- Three host coordinator tests bind a single immutable source snapshot to ranking, reminders,
  start-of-cycle view, privacy decisions, and all eight authority-free executive skills; duplicate
  or incomplete privacy scope and a changes-since view without its prior snapshot fail closed.
- Native Chat, interactive CLI, JSON, SDK, and ACP route the same identity-only portfolio command
  through that coordinator. An exact aggregate binding rejects workspace, record, history, privacy,
  or projection substitution while raw records remain host-owned and external effects stay absent.
