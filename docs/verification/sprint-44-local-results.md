## Deterministic Campaigns

- Six focused unit tests distinguish valid, ambiguous, contradictory, overbroad, and
  missing-context intent and reproduced, unreproduced, inconclusive, unsafe, supported,
  rejected, and forged plan states.
- Five integration tests cover five hostile repository-instruction classes, one
  fictional two-file golden plan, unexplained regression scope, false completion, and
  10,000 deterministic plan mutations with zero verified acceptance.
- Twelve runtime record fixtures, including change-intent and reproduction records,
  pass closed schema and semantic validation. Unknown fields, inherited authority,
  false readiness, and false reproduction outcomes are rejected.
- The hostile canary scan serializes intent, impact, and plan records and finds no
  source canary or instruction text.

These deterministic mutations are not manual or coverage-guided fuzzing.
