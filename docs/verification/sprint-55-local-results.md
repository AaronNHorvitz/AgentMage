verbatim-preserving note and transcript cleanup, evidence-state-aware minutes, explicit unknown
owners and dates, content-free closeout, recurring continuity, local follow-up drafts, four runtime

## Local Campaigns

- Agenda and meeting-request drafts retain purpose, participants, topics, decision needs,
  preparation, expected outputs, and exact source references with no invitation or calendar effect.
- Invitation response and attendance remain independent. Every observed state requires confirmed
  evidence, and silence or the other state cannot supply it.
- Cleanup retains verbatim text, supplied timestamps, attribution confidence and truth state, and
  byte-exact unclear markers while performing no source mutation.
- Minutes distinguish confirmed and proposed decisions and retain source-backed actions,
  questions, risks, and next-meeting details. Missing owners and dates remain absent and unknown.
- Recurring continuity carries open items, preserves immutable history, accepts only exact
  source-backed state updates, and never treats absence as completion.
- The local follow-up remains a draft; invitation, assignment, notification, send, scheduling,
  inbox, network, calendar, and source-mutation effects remain false.
