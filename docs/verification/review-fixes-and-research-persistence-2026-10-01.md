## Change

Review fixes:

- F1: the composed probes send the issuance owner three budget contexts: one of another run of
  the task, one of another session and one under another policy digest. The budget owner
  refuses each as a binding error. A new test sends a valid request for the run's next call
  while the first effect's terminal is pending; the owner refuses it as unusable, and the effect
  then completes.
- N1: Decision 0139 step 7 and the canonical research source document now say that the trusted
  host attests the parent's preview is the plan the person confirmed.
- N2: the owner refuses empty, oversized or digest-mismatched argument bytes before it decodes
  them. A probe with oversized arguments is refused as a call mismatch.
- N8: each composed test's store directory carries the clock in its name. It is removed when the
  test's port is dropped, after the owner closes, also when a test panics.

AMR-03.1.1: the research budget port gains `cancel_research_budget`. Before the coordinator
seals the outcome of an admitted run that observed a cancellation, it has the port cancel the
run's task budget. The cancellation may have been observed between phases, through a cancelled
tool or after a failure that took precedence. The coordinator accepts only the owner's
description of a cancelled budget for the published plan and the admission's scope. Otherwise
the run still ends, and its outcome names `runtime.research.budget_cancellation_unconfirmed`.
The synthetic native worker can now report a failed, timed-out, cancelled or uncertain attempt
after it checks both proofs.

The new tests cover:

- the coordinator's budget cancellation, through synthetic ports: once, after the cancellation
  is observed and before the terminal, both between phases and through a cancelled tool; each
  unconfirmed answer (not cancelled, another plan, another scope, a port failure) named in the
  outcome; and no cancellation for a run that observed none;
- the four new issuance probes and the issuance attempted while an effect is pending;
- through the real coordinator and the real owners:
  - the glue reserves the first request and stops before the start. The reservation is durable
    and the grant unspent. Reopening the store recovers the same revision, head, counts and
    original clocks. The spent request is never reserved again, and a new request counts on
    from the recovered snapshot;
  - after reopening, a rolled-back clock or one at the plan's elapsed limit ends the budget, and
    the end survives another reopening at a valid clock;
  - the glue begins the first effect and stops before the terminal. Reopening closes the
    transaction with the worker's recorded outcome. The grant and the visit stay spent, no
    terminal event names the call, nothing is published and nothing is dispatched again;
  - a worker that fails, times out or becomes uncertain ends the run as failed, exhausted or
    uncertain, under the owner's receipt, and keeps its spent visit across reopening;
  - a cancellation before any request, one while a second request waits for approval and one
    during the effect each cancel the budget durably, with spent visits kept;
  - the plan's elapsed limit passes between issuance and the start. The start's reservation is
    refused, the expiry is retained, nothing starts and the grant stays unspent;
  - a second owner of the same store is refused, and another run of the same task, with the same
    plan or another one, is refused when its budget would open.
