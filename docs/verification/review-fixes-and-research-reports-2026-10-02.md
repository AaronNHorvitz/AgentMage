## Change

Review fixes:

- F1: a synthetic admitted run's model phase observes the cancellation and then reports an
  uncertain, unavailable or exhausted dependency, which outranks it. The outcome names the
  failure's own code, the observation follows the closed turn, and the budget owner is asked
  once, after the observation and before the terminal.
- N1: the canonical research source document says that the owner's `Poisoned` answer during a
  pending effect does not by itself mean damage.
- N2: Decision 0139 step 2 is corrected in place.
- N4: the worker contract says that a failing worker writes no complete frame.

AMR-03.1.4:

- The research budget port gains `publish_research_report`. An admitted run whose verified
  completion is a report draft, in its exact canonical encoding, has it retained through the
  canonical report owner before its terminal.
- The coordinator charges the output and artifact budgets, prepares a run-level report manifest
  of the completing turn without operation, receipt or preview, flushes the journal and asks the
  port. It accepts only the reference its manifest describes, then records the creation event.
  The run's output names the draft.
- An owner refusal ends the run `Failed` with `runtime.research.report_refused`, and nothing is
  retained. Any other port failure is a dependency failure. An unverified draft is never
  retained, and the draft media type outside an admitted run's completion is an invalid
  proposal.
- TASKS.md gains the open row AMR-03.1.4, and the work selector covers 79 amendment rows.
