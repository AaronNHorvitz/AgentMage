AMR-05.9.1 connects the action history component (AMR-05.4) to the coding host:

- The coding tool boundary keeps one entry for each call whose grant it consumed
  and one for each call a person refused or narrowed. A consumed grant authorizes
  its entry by the grant's identity and the digest of the exact grant as issued;
  the decision, preview, binding and receipt digests are evidence. The reason code
  says whether a person approved the call or the session preauthorization covered
  it, which the runtime's evaluation alone does not say.
- The host's live runtime service keeps one entry for each job control request the
  durable job ledger decided, under the client scope it derived from the
  authenticated peer. A retry adds nothing. The history continues across a
  resumption in the same host.
- Run declarations schema 2 carries both histories over IPC wire version 9, and
  the history types parse closed. The variant without authority now refuses a
  member beside its tag; its encoding, and so every entry digest, is unchanged.
- The development CLI drops a history that does not replay to its head or holds
  another owner's kinds. It shows both histories after each run, in text and in
  the JSON declarations row. `--action-history-export CHAIN:FROM:TO` prints the
  redacted, rescanned export of a range on standard output before the outcome
  line; otherwise nothing is exported and nothing is written to a file. The demo
  harness passes the option through.
