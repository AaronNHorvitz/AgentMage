# Conversation Guide

Conversation metadata, immutable turns, references, branches, and checked compactions live in the
encrypted operational store. Local search is bounded. A branch starts from an exact historical
turn and checkpoint without rewriting its parent. Resume blocks when workspace, file,
instruction, repository, citation, model, permission, or policy state has drifted.

Private archives are separately keyed SQLCipher snapshots. Portable evidence bundles are
non-executable derivatives containing only exact approved excerpts and derived references after an
exact disclosure preview. Neither artifact becomes startup or action authority.
